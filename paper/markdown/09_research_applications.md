## Research Applications

This section describes **proposed uses of mathematical components**, not
validated integrations or empirical successes. No domain dataset, controlled
study, deployment or measured benefit is reported. The
[research boundary guide](../../docs/research/index.md) owns detailed limits;
the [API reference](../../docs/api/index.md) owns runnable software interfaces.

### Active Inference and Cognitive Science

Finite probability tables with rational inputs can use exact rational algebra,
including normalized Bayesian updates when the evidence is nonzero. A squared
prediction residual is not complete variational free energy. Likelihoods,
latent-variable models, derivatives, expectations and generally nonrational
logarithms require explicit definitions and methods. No inference engine,
spatial-cognition model or neural architecture is supplied by this section.

### Materials Science and Crystallography

Ideal quadray/FCC geometry could contribute descriptors to a separate material
model. Specify cell convention, embedding, scale, occupancy and uncertainty.
Coordination counts and polyhedral volumes do not establish material stability,
space-group refinement or optimized properties. Such conclusions need physical
models, measured data and comparisons.

### Biological Pattern Recognition

Geometry of a discretized molecular model is not a folding-energy model or
clinical predictor. `from_xyz` quantizes continuous coordinates to integer
sites; preserve original observations and quantify that error. Digit-pattern
utilities are not sequence-alignment, genetic-discovery or protein-analysis
pipelines.

### Environmental and Climate Modeling

Arithmetic accounting and spatial geometry may be subcomponents of a study.
They do not implement climate dynamics, geological mechanics, ecosystem health
or carbon optimization. Measured benefit requires an identified model, data,
calibration, uncertainty and an appropriate counterfactual.

### Complex Systems and Network Analysis

A graph or geometric representation does not supply a dynamical law. Define
nodes, edges, state, update rule and conservation properties before interpreting
transport, emergence or phase transitions. No general network-analysis or
anomaly-detection efficacy is claimed here.

### Educational Applications

Rational arithmetic and visualization can support mathematical activities.
Improved understanding or retention is a hypothesis requiring an educational
study, not an implication of numerical exactness. See
[learning activities and evaluation design](../../docs/learning-theory-synergetics.md).
