# Research Documentation: Claim and Source Discipline

Maintain [index.md](index.md) as the research-scope hub, linked by
[README.md](README.md). These are hand-written source documents.
Link callable details to [API contracts](../api/index.md) rather than inventing
domain APIs. Distinguish exact rational operations, approximations, geometric
assumptions, hypotheses and empirical evidence. No benchmark or success story
without identifiable inputs, method, comparator and results.
